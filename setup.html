2Your Wavelog

Where your log lives. Find or create an API key in Wavelog under your account menu → API Keys (a read-only key is enough).

3Email alerts (optional)

Watch your Gmail for new ARRL DX Bulletins and email you what you need. Your mailbox is only read: nothing is marked, moved or deleted.

  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification for your Google account.
  2. Create an app password at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords and paste it here. Your normal Gmail password won't work.
  3. Make sure IMAP is on in Gmail → Settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP.

4Admin password

You'll use this to open the checker. At least 8 characters.

Settings are saved inside the container's data volume, readable only by the app's own unprivileged user. Your keys are never shown again or sent back to the browser, and your admin password is stored only as a salted hash.
